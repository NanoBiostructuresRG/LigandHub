📖 About LigandHub

Supported inputs: SDF, PDB, MOL2, SMILES strings

Output: PDBQT format ready for AutoDock, AutoDock Vina, or Smina

Processing: Hydrogen addition at user-specified pH, charge assignment, torsion tree detection, and conversion to PDBQT
